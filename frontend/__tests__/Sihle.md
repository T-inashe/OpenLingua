## User Report: Sihle

Sihle highlighted how approachable the lessons felt, even after long breaks. Streak recovery and gentle reminders helped re‑engage without guilt. Interactive dialogues made speaking practice less intimidating.

Highlights:
- Crisp visuals and consistent iconography
- Dialogue branching kept sessions fresh
- Review queue balanced old and new content

Room for improvement:
- Better sync between web and mobile lesson positions
- Option to pin favourite decks to the top

Summary: Friendly, forgiving UX that still drives steady progress.
