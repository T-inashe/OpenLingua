## User Report: Tinashe

Overall, using the platform felt smooth and intuitive. The onboarding flow made it clear what to do next, and the daily streak feature was motivating. Tinashe especially liked the spaced-repetition practice for tricky vocabulary and found the listening exercises realistic without being overwhelming.

Highlights:
- Quick sign-in and clear dashboard layout
- Pronunciation feedback felt helpful, not nitpicky
- Progress charts made improvement visible week-to-week

Room for improvement:
- Occasional lag switching between lessons on slower Wi‑Fi
- Would love more idioms in the intermediate modules

Summary: Confident recommending the platform to friends starting at an A2–B1 level.
